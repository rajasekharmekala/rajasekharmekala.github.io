Adobe Brand Concierge — LLM Agent Evaluation & Optimization Platform

Adobe Inc. • Primary Creator
Designed and built the full-stack evaluation and prompt-optimization platform supporting enterprise deployments across semiconductors & hardware, enterprise SaaS, retail, travel & hospitality, and sports/entertainment.
  • Full-Stack Evaluation Studio: Built the FastAPI backend and React operator UI supporting offline experiment runs, online span evaluation, knowledge-base exploration, and Okta-authenticated multi-tenant workflows.
  • GEPA Reflective Prompt & Judge Optimization: Integrated Genetic-Pareto Prompt Optimization (GEPA) with multi-profile reasoning models, reflection loops, tunable multi-judge rubrics, and a Judge Correlation Matrix.
  • Durable Staged Persistence: Engineered chunked SQL Server staging tables, bulk row ingestion, paginated retrieval, and replay diagnostics scaling up to 50,000-answer evaluation datasets.
  • Grounding & Guardrail Diagnostics: Built offline source-attribution calibration and guardrail analysis views to compare assistant variants and inspect execution traces end-to-end.

EchoPrompt (NAACL 2024) — Instructing the Model to Rephrase Queries for Improved In-Context Learning and Chain-of-Thought Reasoning

UC Irvine NLP Lab • Importance: 2
EchoPrompt is a simple yet effective approach that prompts language models to rephrase their queries before answering them. Published in Findings of NAACL 2024 with Yasaman Razeghi and Dr. Sameer Singh at UC Irvine.
  • Evaluated across four causal language model families on numerical reasoning (GSM8K, SVAMP), reading comprehension (DROP), and logical reasoning tasks.
  • Improves Zero-Shot-CoT accuracy on code-davinci-002 by 5% on numerical reasoning and 13% on reading comprehension.
  • Demonstrates that both the original query and the model-generated rephrased version provide complementary semantic signals that stabilize in-context learning.
