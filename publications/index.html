NAACL 2024 Selected EchoPrompt: Instructing the Model to Rephrase Queries for Improved In-context Learning
Rajasekhar Reddy Mekala, Yasaman Razeghi, and Sameer Singh
Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: NAACL 2024
PDF arXiv:2309.10687
Abstract & BibTeX
We introduce EchoPrompt, a simple yet effective approach that prompts the language model to rephrase its queries before answering them. Just like humans, reminding the model of the question at hand by rephrasing it seems to help preserve context and reinforce connections across tokens. Through extensive experimentation with four causal model families across various tasks—including numerical reasoning (GSM8K, SVAMP, MultiArith, SingleOp), reading comprehension (DROP), and logical reasoning (tracking shuffled objects)—we demonstrate that EchoPrompt introduces substantial improvements in zero-shot and few-shot in-context learning performance without requiring any additional training or external tools. Specifically, EchoPrompt improves Zero-Shot-CoT performance on code-davinci-002 by 5% on numerical reasoning and 13% on reading comprehension tasks on average, and yields consistent gains across GPT-3.5-turbo, Llama-2, and Starcoder.

EMNLP 2022 Selected Snoopy: An Online Interface for Exploring the Effect of Pretraining Term Frequencies on Few-Shot LM Performance
Yasaman Razeghi*, Raja Sekhar Reddy Mekala*, Robert L. Logan IV, Matt Gardner, and Sameer Singh
Proceedings of the 2022 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing: System Demonstrations (EMNLP 2022), pp. 389–395
PDF ACL Anthology
Abstract & BibTeX
SNOOPY is an online interface for exploring Pile term frequencies alongside precomputed performance of EleutherAI/GPT models on more than 20 NLP benchmarks. It lets users inspect how term frequency relates to few-shot model accuracy across model sizes, example counts, and datasets.
