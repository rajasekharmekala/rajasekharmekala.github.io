I am a Senior Software & Machine Learning Systems Engineer at Adobe (Santa Clara, CA), working on core Generative AI platforms and multi-tenant LLM agent runtimes for Adobe Brand Concierge (and previously Adobe Journey Optimizer). Prior to Adobe, I earned my Master’s in Computer Science from the University of California, Irvine (GPA: 3.94/4.00), where I researched in-context learning, prompt robustness, and chain-of-thought reasoning in LLMs under Dr. Sameer Singh. Earlier in my career, I engineered real-time IoT vehicle telemetry microservices and compliance systems at TruckX, built search autocomplete and analytics microservices at Adobe TechComm (2018–2021), and conducted cloud performance research at Adobe Research’s BigData Experience Lab (2017), winning the Best Intern Project Award and co-authoring US Patent US20190354388.

Outside of engineering, I am an avid sports enthusiast with a strong passion for badminton, a keen follower of equity markets, and a lifelong believer in forming rigorous hypotheses and validating them with empirical data.

NAACL 2024 Selected EchoPrompt: Instructing the Model to Rephrase Queries for Improved In-Context Learning
Rajasekhar Reddy Mekala, Yasaman Razeghi, and Sameer Singh
Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: NAACL 2024
PDF arXiv:2309.10687
Abstract & BibTeX
We introduce EchoPrompt, a simple yet effective approach that prompts the language model to rephrase its queries before answering them. Just like humans, reminding the model of the question at hand by rephrasing it seems to help preserve context and reinforce connections across tokens. Through extensive experimentation with four causal model families across numerical reasoning (GSM8K, SVAMP), reading comprehension (DROP), and logical reasoning tasks, EchoPrompt yields substantial improvements (e.g., +5% on numerical reasoning and +13% on reading comprehension in Zero-Shot-CoT with code-davinci-002).

EMNLP 2022 Selected Snoopy: An Online Interface for Exploring the Effect of Pretraining Term Frequencies on Few-Shot LM Performance
Yasaman Razeghi*, Raja Sekhar Reddy Mekala*, Robert L. Logan IV, Matt Gardner, and Sameer Singh
Proceedings of the 2022 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing: System Demonstrations (EMNLP 2022)
PDF ACL Anthology
Abstract
SNOOPY is an online interface for exploring Pile term frequencies alongside precomputed model performance across NLP benchmarks. It lets users inspect how term frequency relates to few-shot accuracy across models, example counts, and datasets.
